# Changelog

All notable changes to LocalConvert are documented here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [0.1.0-beta] — 2026-10-22

The first public beta. 🎉 Convert files on your own computer, with nothing uploaded.

### Added
- **90+ input file types:** images (including iPhone HEIC and AVIF), PDF, Word, text and Markdown, spreadsheets and data (XLSX, CSV, JSON, YAML), audio, video, archives and subtitles.
- **Click wheel:** a round, iPod-style widget that floats over your windows. Drag a file onto it, glide to a format and release to convert, or drop it in the centre and spin. It plays a soft click as you move between formats.
- **Glass UI:** a frosted-glass main window that follows light and dark mode, and turns solid when *Reduce transparency* is on.
- **Batch conversion** with *Convert all to*, and **combine** images, PDFs and documents into one PDF.
- **Photo privacy:** GPS location and camera details are removed by default when converting photos.
- **Exact-layout Office conversions** when LibreOffice is installed (optional).
- **Command line:** `convert`, `formats` and `doctor`.
- Local-only server: bound to `127.0.0.1`, a secret token per launch, a `Host` check and a strict Content-Security-Policy.
- Standalone apps for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Windows, with FFmpeg bundled so audio and video work out of the box.
- App icon.
